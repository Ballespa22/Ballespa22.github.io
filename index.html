Real-time audio recorder on an FPGA

For the MSc I built a recorder and player that runs in real time on a Zybo Z7-20. The audio path lives in programmable logic, written in VHDL with Vivado and Simulink. The ARM core on the same chip configures the audio codec in C. Buttons and switches on the board set the volume and switch between recording and playback.

VHDL · Vivado · Vitis · Simulink · C

Feb – May 2026MSc Deep Learning

Neural networks on an FPGA

On an Ultra96 board I took networks from a training script all the way to the FPGA's DPU with Vitis AI. First an MNIST classifier trained in TensorFlow 2, quantized to 8-bit integers and compiled. Then a ResNet-50 for ImageNet, from both TensorFlow and PyTorch. The course behind it covered why this works: accelerator architectures, systolic arrays, pruning, quantization and memory bottlenecks.

Vitis AI · DPU · TensorFlow · PyTorch · Zynq UltraScale+

A drone with its own eyes in progress

A partner and I are building a multi-camera drone from nothing: frame, motors, battery and power, electronics and a 4K camera rig. We don't split the work; we both do all of it. Sep 2024 – Jun 2025bachelor's thesis

Spotting cyberattacks in network traffic

My bachelor's thesis compared deep learning models for intrusion detection on CIC-IDS2018: more than 16 million network flows in 13 classes, from DDoS to SQL injection. Most of the work was the data: feature selection, normalization, and rebalancing the rare attacks with SMOTE. A CNN that reads each flow as a small image reached 86% accuracy and 0.87 weighted F1. It beat the MLP but took ten times longer to train, so I proposed using both: a light filter first, deep analysis after.

TensorFlow · Keras · focal loss · SMOTE

Jul 2026 – nowMSc thesis · Eldarion

Teaching an agent to trade intraday in progress

My MSc thesis, done with Eldarion Solutions: an agent that trades the S&P 500 ETF every five minutes. It is trained with PPO and only judged after transaction costs, with walk-forward validation over several seeds. Most of my time goes into ablations, seeing what changes when the reward, the features or the training setup change. Next step: several assets at once.

reinforcement learning · PPO · backtesting · Sharpe ratio

Nov 2025 – Jan 2026MSc Telecom

Can market mood predict prices?

I gathered Fed statements, news, tweets, StockTwits and Reddit posts, turned them into sentiment with FinBERT and RoBERTa models, and combined them with price and macro data in XGBoost, LSTM and SVM models, validated walk-forward and explained with SHAP. The honest answer: for the S&P 500 as a whole, barely better than a coin. For single stocks like Apple or Tesla, a test AUC between 0.62 and 0.72.

FinBERT · RoBERTa · XGBoost · SHAP · Optuna
